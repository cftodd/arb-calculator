Average odds
—
Average odds is weighted by stake size (larger bets count more toward the average). Payout = stake + profit. Positive odds: profit = stake × odds/100. Negative odds: profit = stake × 100/|odds|.

Combined implied probability
—
Stakes are split proportional to each bet's implied probability so both outcomes return the same payout. Combined implied probability under 100% means a true arbitrage (guaranteed profit); over 100% means a guaranteed loss.
